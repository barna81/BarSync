Flash BarSync Firmware

Right from your browser — no Arduino IDE, no driver install. Works in Chrome or Edge (desktop, Windows/macOS/Linux/ChromeOS).

  1. Connect BarSync to your computer via USB
  2. Click "Install" below and select the matching serial port
  3. Wait for flashing to finish — the device restarts automatically

Checking firmware version…

Your browser doesn't support WebSerial. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer — or flash the classic way via the Arduino IDE (see the README).
